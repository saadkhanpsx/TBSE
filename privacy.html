Our commitment to your privacy

The Book Signing Events (“TBSE,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you complete a form on our site, you provide:

  • Your full name, email address, and phone number
  • Your city and state
  • Whether you are a published author
  • For applications: your book’s title, a link to your book, and a short introduction of yourself as an author
  • Any other details you choose to share with us

During our services, we may also receive information about your author profile, book, website, and social channels, as well as photos and video captured at your event.

Like most websites, we may receive basic technical information, such as browser type, device, and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange a consultation
  • To evaluate your author profile and recommend the right package
  • To deliver our services, including pitching your book and coordinating your event
  • To send updates about your project and, where you agree, news about our services
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our website and services
  • To meet legal and contractual obligations

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to deliver our services and run our business:

  • Barnes & Noble and event partners. We share the details needed to pitch and coordinate your event, such as your name, book information, and availability.
  • Service providers. Trusted vendors that help us operate, such as website hosting, form handling, email, scheduling, photography, and videography, who may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Event photos and video

Photos and video of your event are created for you and delivered to you. How TBSE may feature event media in its portfolio and marketing is covered by your signed service agreement.

Cookies and third-party services

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality and, if enabled, analytics. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver our services, and meet legal, tax, and contractual requirements, and then delete or anonymize it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect what you share with us.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing messages at any time. Depending on where you live, such as California, you may have additional privacy rights, and we will honor valid requests as required by applicable law. To make a request, contact us at info@thebooksigningevents.com.

Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time, and the date above shows the latest revision. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@thebooksigningevents.com.
