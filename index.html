Your author journey

A proven two-phase program, from author to signing day.

For 12 years, every author at TBSE has followed one refined path: build a presence that bookstores take seriously, then deliver a signing day readers remember. Two phases. One clear destination.

Phase 1

Build a presence that earns approval

What we do. We begin with a complete evaluation of your author profile, your book’s presence, and every promotion you have run to date. From that, we design a precise strategy and put the right platforms and services to work: a stronger profile, a growing following, a polished author website, your book available at Barnes & Noble and across additional platforms in every format readers want, and active social media promotion ahead of your event.

Why it works. Barnes & Noble approves authors who look ready. Phase 1 builds exactly that, with an audience already forming long before your event is scheduled.

  • Profile & book evaluation
  • Website & platforms
  • Social marketing
See everything Phase 1 covers →
Phase 2

Secure your store and own the day

What we do. When our event coordination team confirms that you and your book are ready, a dedicated event coordinator joins your team, reviews every detail, and pitches your book to your nearest hosting Barnes & Noble. Approval can take days, weeks, or months. If a store declines, we uncover the reason, strengthen the case, and reapply.

Why it works. Once the store confirms, we run the entire experience. You choose the date and time. We promote continuously, staff the store, set up your table, manage every formality, and capture the day on camera.

Questions authors ask

Clear answers, before you begin.

What does TBSE do?

TBSE builds your author presence and takes you to a Barnes & Noble signing in two phases. Phase 1 develops your profile, website, book availability, and promotion. Phase 2 pitches your book, secures your date, and runs your event.

How do the two phases work?

In Phase 1 we evaluate your profile, book, and past promotion, then build and promote. When our event coordination team confirms you are ready, Phase 2 begins: a dedicated event coordinator pitches your book to the nearest B&N store and, once confirmed, prepares your event.

Is a bookstore signing guaranteed?

Barnes & Noble makes every approval decision independently, so no agency can promise one. What we do is prepare you thoroughly, pitch professionally, and reapply when needed.

How does pricing and payment work?

Pricing is shared after a consultation with our expert. You pay an upfront Phase 1 fee, which covers your author development work and is not payment for a guaranteed Barnes & Noble event. A separate fixed $100 event coordination fee is charged only after Barnes & Noble approves your event. If it is not approved, you are never asked for it.

How long does B&N approval take?

It can take a few days, a few weeks, or sometimes months. Timing is set by the store, and we follow up throughout.

What if B&N declines?

We find out the reason, address it, and reapply. Everything completed in Phase 1 stays yours.

Who chooses the event date?

You do. The day, date, and time slot are chosen around your availability, at the nearest participating store that can host.

What happens on event day?

Our team is at the store with you. We set up your table, handle your prints, and manage formalities with store representatives, after promoting continuously beforehand to gather your audience.

Do I receive photos and video?

Yes. Our photographer and videographer capture your event, and you receive professionally edited photos and videos.

Is there a sales analysis?

The Elite Author Package includes a complete analysis of your book’s sales before and after the event.

Who owns the project work?

Project-specific work is intended to remain yours under the signed service agreement once applicable obligations and payments are fulfilled. TBSE does not claim ongoing ownership of your completed manuscript or book.

How do I get started?

Send your application. A TBSE expert will contact you for a consultation, review your goals, and recommend the right package before any work begins.
