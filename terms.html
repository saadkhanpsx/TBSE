Barnes & Noble independently controls store participation, approvals, locations, schedules, and availability.

Our two-phase program

TBSE delivers its services in two phases.

Phase 1, author and book development. This is the primary paid service. We evaluate your publishing status, book presentation, professional profile, online presence, branding, visibility, marketing assets, and other relevant factors. Any reasonable gaps we identify are addressed through the services included in your selected package, which may include:

  • Barnes & Noble listing and publishing support, and applicable multi-platform publishing or distribution
  • Author profile, website, social media presence, and Google Knowledge Panel assistance, if included
  • Book trailer, promotional creatives, marketing, and visibility support, if included
  • Author branding, book presentation, positioning strategy, and ongoing project guidance

The goal of Phase 1 is to strengthen you and your book to a level TBSE reasonably considers ready for bookstore opportunity coordination.

Phase 2, the Barnes & Noble opportunity. Once Phase 1 is substantially completed and your profile is considered ready, we present you for consideration for a Barnes & Noble book-signing opportunity and, if approved, coordinate your event.

The specific services you receive depend on the package you select and are confirmed with you before any work begins.

Store approval and no guarantee

Final approval, participating store, location, scheduling, and availability remain entirely at Barnes & Noble’s discretion. TBSE cannot guarantee that any store will approve, schedule, or host an event, or the timing of any decision. Past performance does not constitute a guarantee of approval, and approval can take days, weeks, or months.

We manage the related communication and coordination with the participating store on your behalf. Once an opportunity is approved, we work to arrange your event at a mutually suitable date and time, based on store availability and your convenience.

If a first attempt is declined or delayed, we may review any remaining positioning gaps and re-attempt the opportunity at a later stage. A decline or delay in Phase 2 does not, by itself, mean that Phase 1 was unsuccessful or undelivered.

Pricing, fees, and payment

  • Package pricing is provided after a consultation with a TBSE expert and is confirmed in writing before work begins.
  • Phase 1 fee (upfront). The upfront fee pays for the Phase 1 work and deliverables. It is not payment for a guaranteed Barnes & Noble event.
  • Approval and event coordination fee. A fixed $100 fee is charged only after Barnes & Noble approves or confirms your participation opportunity. It is not charged upfront and is not due if approval is not obtained. Detailed event preparation begins once it is paid.

Refund policy and separation of phases

If you are dissatisfied with the quality of a delivered Phase 1 product or service, you may request a refund. We will review your request and process any applicable refund accordingly.

The Phase 1 fee is not refundable solely because of the result of Phase 2, including a Barnes & Noble decline, delay, scheduling limitation, or unsuccessful placement attempt, provided the agreed Phase 1 services have been delivered.

Event day support

Once your event is confirmed, our team supports you before, during, and after the day. We promote the event to gather your audience, and our team is present at the store to assist you, set up your table, manage prints, and handle formalities with store representatives. Store sales systems and in-store policies remain under the store’s control.
